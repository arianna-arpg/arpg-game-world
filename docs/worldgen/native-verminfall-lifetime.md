# Retained Verminfall event lifetime

A completed infestation can naturally return to farmland that is already retained.
The first repair made successor bodies appear on that ground, but its per-event
marker reconciliation also allowed another pour during the same visit. Ordinary
play keeps its materialization guard until the next zone visit. A paired check on
172be2a0 demonstrated the difference: after genuine nest/king kills and natural
same-zone reignition, one actual frame created zero new nests/discovery in ordinary
play and two nests plus one discovery in retained play. Both lanes used the same
first and second events and the same bounded isolated field-clock steps.

## Visit and event ownership

Placement is consumed for the current visit. A changed event cannot reopen that
consumed guard while a player stays, while a remote peer remains, or while the
area is empty. A genuine warm arrival may renew placement only when no actual
physical seat outside the arriving cohort occupies the destination. Downed hands
still occupy it. The coordinator validates the moving frame, actual registered
area, seat, body, metadata and physical membership; publication or a fabricated
occupancy flag is insufficient.

The coordinator selects the Verminfall arrival callable before issuing its private
invocation. The lifetime claims that invocation once and can validate it repeatedly
through the synchronous call. Reentry and sibling calls cannot borrow the stage;
return, throw and an asynchronous result revoke it before later arrival hooks.
The new call follows the existing Warlord arrival and precedes Vendetta. The
relative hook order is preserved; this is not a claim of complete ordinary-load
RNG equivalence.

A continuing event retains its bodies, wounds and consumed marker. Existing
logical infestation IDs distinguish a successor from a same-ID row reconstructed
by restore on the same field. Captured field/row objects still authenticate each
selected operation; matching IDs do not grant body or mutation authority. A
changed or absent event on a genuine unoccupied arrival releases only this
package's marker and invokes its native materializer synchronously. If no event
exists, a later first eligible claim follows the existing unconsumed-marker path.
First births in previously unconsumed empty resident areas remain separate
pre-existing behavior; this change does not establish an empty-area policy.

Surviving vermin, unrelated bodies and other package markers remain. Nest and king
operations stay attached to their genuine issuing event and construction. The
ordinary unbound World path keeps its original calls. No save schema is added.

## Selected operations and partial failures

The owner captures the actual overlay helpers, combat entry/hooks and population
construction receipts. Query, field, factory and insertion operations validate
before selection, before invocation and after invocation. Queries retain their
native position before the materialization guard. A factory result must have
been constructed by that invocation; an earlier unused body or copied package
tag cannot authorize an event payout.

Completed native effects survive exceptions. A marker deletion that completes
before throwing stays deleted and leaves that visit eligible for a later native
attempt. A marker add consumes placement at the add boundary even when a later
factory deletes it and throws. Inserted/admitted partial bodies remain; a later
live pass cannot replay their consumed placement. The first selected exception
is preserved, the arrival capability expires, and subsequent arrival hooks do
not run after an unhandled failure.

Nest death and a completed field decrement remain if king spawning throws.
Warm visits do not fabricate a missing king after that consumed birth. If the
selected spawn inserts and admits a body but throws before returning, that body
stays without invented king ownership. A genuine king clear may finish its
original reward/notice after ending its own event, once only. Nested public scopes
cannot redirect an old body's mutation into a replacement event.

The historical direct Crusade live-registry entry still composes once through
the actual Verminfall owner. A private synchronous recognition scope prevents a
second registry invocation. This preserves the earlier marker replay and nested
kill corrections while changing when a successor placement is eligible.

## Verification and explicit test supersession

The original `check_nativeverminfallboundaries.ts` source and its 35-case result
remain historical evidence. Two expectations in it were incorrect for visit
parity: a remote occupant received a successor without leaving, and the active
direct-Crusade case required another nest cohort during that visit. Its standalone
CI invocation was first superseded by `check_nativeverminfallarrivalboundaries.ts`:
25 trigger-independent cases, eight placement faults staged at genuine warm
arrivals, two corrected timing expectations, and eleven additional arrival
controls. The sealed probe roster and older source archives remain unchanged.

The current CI entry is `check_nativeverminfallcrusadeboundaries.ts` (48 cases).
Its committed contract in `balance/probesupersessioncontracts.ts` maps all 46
previous cases, preserves the first 45 case bodies and all five original final
assertions, and records the actual fixture-order failure digest. The final fault
now observes Crusade post-return validation before entering the intended
Verminfall stage; two additional controls cover both leaves' post-return windows.
`check_probesupersessionscurrent.ts` checks source identities, this mapping and
the exact CI invocation. The older 46-case source is historical evidence.

`check_nativeverminfallarrival.ts` covers visit timing against current gameplay;
`check_nativeverminfall.ts` retains the ordinary/retained natural successor and
reward comparison. Both cold modes in `check_nativeverminfallcold.ts` use isolated
launcher disk saves and fresh-process ordinary/retained Continue for remaining
nests and an armed king. The current arrival-source checker preserves exact
predecessor reads for older source proofs; those projections are never used as
current gameplay acceptance.

Earlier 172be2a0 diagnostic and bc62b9a5 natural, 35-boundary and cold results
remain evidence for their exact sources; they do not override the demonstrated
same-visit regression. Verify the successor against the paired visit and complete
46-case courses, current source composition, all four type lanes, affected
retained travel/arrival and host checks, fresh cold courses and explicit hosted
CI entries before promotion.

Fixtures disclose skipped prologue, staged level/invulnerability, direct native
travel/kills/wounds, isolated field-clock advancement where used, and selected
callback replacement in fault cases. Actual local Seat/road occupancy is distinct
from TCP/browser/dormancy acceptance. Package reward comparisons fix only their
package-row RNG and normalize item IDs/drop positions. Direct restore replacement
is a fault seam; stale-body policy differences there are not asserted to be
normal live gameplay. Full content parity, migration and capacity remain open.

## Performance boundary

The lifetime wrapper adds an actor-descriptor copy and physical ownership entries
to each live-registry pass. Each resident area runs that pass per completed
simulation frame, including empty areas. Before existing Crusade work, the wrapper
has four ownership entries; a present field adds three query checks and a consumed
active event adds one placement check. Entry and exit both validate. The arrival
correction adds validation at the travel boundary and does not introduce a per-frame
census cache or remove callback checks.

These are source counts, not measured savings. A separate bulk-descriptor walk
candidate passed 25 equivalence cases but was about 1.75-1.83 times slower at all
four clean census sizes in its paired microbenchmark and was rejected. That result
neither accepts a different shortcut nor establishes game performance. Any future
reduction must preserve selected-callback invalidation, first-error order and
current physical ownership, and pass a calibrated comparison on the actual host.
