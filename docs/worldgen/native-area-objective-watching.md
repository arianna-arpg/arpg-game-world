# Area-owned quests, progression and objective watching

The ongoing area composition connects the actual quest, progression and objective
owners to one retained assembly. Objective completion calls the area's quest owner;
quest XP rebuilds the recipient through exact actor authority. Recipient rewards
follow the native selected hand to that hand's physical area. Cargo, rescue bodies,
objective effects and ongoing objective state remain with the source area.
Campaign quest books, account receipts, graph history and save-writer identity
remain shared.

## Original behavior and ownership

`nativeSceneQuests` shares 31 original World methods. `nativeQuestOdyssey` shares
11 original quest-facing Odyssey methods, and rescues use the original
`QuestRescues` implementation. The sole quest receiver projection is the actual
campaign character writer supplied to `saveCharacter`. The retained owner keeps
live quest scope through nested bounty lookups and restores temporary scope on
errors. It uses actual physical drops, native reward choices and native graph
policies.

`nativeSceneProgression` shares 29 original methods. It uses the original
GuardArts, CompanionBonds, AttackSequences, Challenges, CompanionGrants and
LitePool implementations. Pure bar, wallet and saved-skill reconstruction helpers
are shared without a runtime dependency on the character writer. Native partial
mutation and failure ordering remain intact.

`nativeSceneObjectiveWatching` shares 19 original methods. The original geographic
dispatch remains at World's entry seam. Native clear, boss, bounty, puzzle, lair,
package, venture, offering, hold, beacon, escape, wave and procession behavior
uses the area's actual population, physical objects, geometry and completion.

Source fixtures check exact delegates, live host members and helper declarations
before restoring older historical source. They retain the original complete World
hashes at 55f73fb6 and 42daac58 and main's audited Miasma expiry lineage. Unrelated
changes cannot be hidden by the normalization chain.

`NativeAreaActorAuthority` uses exact body and scene identities. Matching IDs or
coordinates do not establish ownership. Claims are checked before mutation;
transfers require explicit physical destination membership. Carried hero husks
resolve through their exact live seat and controlled body. A borrowed preparation
observer cannot authorize gameplay in the neighboring scene.

`NativeAreaSceneBirthAdmission` tracks exact bodies returned by the actual local
population factory. It admits only recorded bodies inserted by native watcher or
rescue operations, including nested and delayed callbacks. Native partial effects
survive an exception; arbitrary census additions never acquire authority through
a blanket sweep.

`NativeAreaScenePresence` distinguishes the fixed preparation cohort from admitted
physical attendance. After activation, objective credit, spoil allocation and
quest attendance read the same live authority. Campaign ledger lookup remains
available for absent players without granting them local attendance.

`NativeAreaSceneOngoing` constructs all three ongoing owners and the required
combat owner, registers their exact scene, and connects the assembly's completion
callbacks to the actual owners. One script lifecycle is shared with residents and
rescues. Admission follows completed native birth and an explicit physical census;
construction itself performs no movement, terrain publication or save registration.

## Remaining activation work

The production combat runtime, movement/scheduling, complete spatial publication
and whole-area persistence remain required. The composition requires real owners
and has no active-World fallback. Native summon construction rebuilds a newborn
before insertion; that path needs an explicit staged construction capability.
Detached effect-caster lifetimes that can still deal damage also need retained
runtime authority. Companion grant rows must transfer with their actual owner.

Choice and imbue prompts still need recipient-aware client presentation; the
retained quest flags alone do not prove delivery through the active UI.

Objective attendance currently requires a real local representative; a scheduler
must not substitute a player located in another area or silently invent a pause
policy. Keep the actual mutable census: filtering a temporary array loses native
births and removals.

Bounded and open layouts, walls, barriers, mountains and meaningful routes follow
complete content ownership and publication. They will shape movement through the
same continuous world, with internal area identities supporting objectives and
persistence rather than player-facing border teleports.
