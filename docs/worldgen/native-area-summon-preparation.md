# Native area summon preparation

NativeSceneSummonPreparation shares the original summon-pool selection and
support-forwarding rules. NativeAreaSceneSummonPreparation permits the original
owner-stat and level preparation on bodies minted by the area's actual native
population factory, before those bodies enter the physical census.

## Preparation and physical admission

A preparation operation requires the actual admitted caster and the identical
registered progression owner. A synchronous scope records the precise bodies
returned by that population's original factory operation. A matching actor ID,
position, caster pointer or user-supplied factory does not establish that receipt.
The scope rejects bodies already inserted locally or claimed by area authority.
It does not insert or claim a body itself.

Preparation may forward support gems to a minted body's real skills and apply
native owner-stat inheritance and releveling. The existing progression derivations
remain authoritative: actual account and local-seat relic power, owner attributes,
throng and hive rules, life fractions, skill levels, native random consumption
and allocation order are preserved. Provider lookup and invocation keep their
actual receivers and original evaluation order.

Each population has a private synchronous scope stack. A nested operation cannot
use an outer scope until the inner operation returns. Returning or throwing
expires that scope; later calls cannot mint or prepare another body with it.
A failed operation retains native effects that already happened, including
allocated identities and consumed random values. It grants no lasting preparation
permission and adds no rollback policy to the native algorithm.

Returning an asynchronous or thenable result is refused immediately. Its captured
then method is observed after the scope expires, with the same receiver and one
lookup, so catching that refusal cannot leave a later unhandled rejection.
This does not make asynchronous preparation supported.

The nonmutating authority check reads actual registered ownership. It does not
claim to detect a body copied into an arbitrary unregistered external array.
Physical publication must still keep every scene's real census authoritative.

## Remaining construction work

This is preparation, not complete spawnMinion or spawnConstruct. Original cap
eviction and death cleanup, auras, impact damage and the exact insertion/admission
boundary remain necessary. A later full spawn must admit a new body where the
native algorithm inserts it, before subsequent aura, tether or impact effects.
It must not claim an entire census after the operation has finished.

Detached effect casters, ongoing summon timers, crossing, scheduling and whole-area
client publication remain separate lifecycle work. Do not substitute an empty
combat provider or bypass physical admission to connect these preparation rules.
