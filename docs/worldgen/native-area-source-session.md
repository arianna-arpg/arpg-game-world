# Native campaign sources and fresh area assembly

Checkpoint: 2026-10-09, after native-area-graph-preparation.md.

## Final architecture

Seamless is the intended sole game/content architecture. Main is the reference
for complete native content and behavior, not a requirement to maintain two game
modes or preserve border teleports, player resets, or visible zones. Keep the
old implementation as a backup branch. Internal native area identities may keep
owning content, events and history while presentation becomes continuous terrain.
Generation integration remains the scope; new visible mechanisms must communicate
through the world and the existing visual language.

## Concrete source session

NativeAreaSourceSession binds a concrete NativeAreaSceneGraph and
MassNativeGeography to the same actual campaign maps, simulation and manifest.
It verifies their identity, campaign seed, dimension and exact captured geography
source. issue takes only an existing campaign ID. It accepts no supplied definition,
seed, face, certificate or substitute manifest. Tickets are local capabilities
and cannot be forged, moved between sessions or persisted across restoration.

Complete landmark complexes retain every actual child object and native ID in
campaign insertion order. The installed program version, stage coverage and
internal reciprocal links are checked; missing or replaced members refuse the
source. Unknown historical program versions refuse rather than silently remint.
The current native and physical positions are candidates, not reservations.
Tickets intentionally follow live campaign definitions; they are not immutable
content snapshots. Geometry-source equality does not certify every executable
layout/population registry or establish a complete installed-build certificate.

chartNeighbors runs the complete original chartNeighborsOf operation, including
road budgets, self/duplicate consolidation, child publication, allocator and event
notifications. It preserves partial native effects on failure and never retries
automatically. Campaign policies are scoped synchronously and restored in finally.

## Fresh owner assembly

NativeAreaSceneAssembly constructs the complete concrete native owner graph from
actual campaign, content-source and service bundles. It does not copy a World
scene, geometry, census, population, resident director or controller state. All
124 birth ports and the complete 36-row runtime registry bind to the new owners.
The arena helper retains the entire original shape and dormant-annex body.

Area geometry, census, population, objective state, physical contents, ecology
and event state are independent. Campaign squad allocation, vendor stock, theater
visit counts and dialogue variation history stay shared. NativeResidentHistory
lets separate directors retain the same variation history; the ordinary director
constructor still defaults to its original fresh map. Service views preserve
live properties, assignment and method receivers, including prototype methods.
Production construction supplies no no-op or throwing substitute services.

prepare validates its own session/definition, runs native boundaries, generates
exactly one layout and adopts it. It privately retains that exact layout, memory
and RNG continuation. Its public result contains only copied frozen entry and
optional spawn positions plus the layout seed. It neither marks a campaign visit
nor teleports/resets the player. The caller establishes the real player stand on
the adopted geometry before birth.

birth checks the full source-family fingerprint and generation continuation, then
runs the complete native birth. Successful owners remain born; preparation or
birth faults preserve partial effects and permanently fail that assembly. A
failed pre-birth validation has no birth effects and leaves preparation intact.
Do not construct an area again because a rendering/streaming chunk returns.
Runtime publication must provide one authoritative retained owner per area.

## Next integration boundary

This checkpoint is production preparation, not playable full-content activation.
The test campaign/source adapter is intentionally test-only. Still required:

1. Extract the real installed source-provider/bootstrap bundle from World so
   production assembly receives full registered native sources without a test
   fixture. Issue new sources through the actual complete campaign graph.
2. Reserve complete native terrain/geometry and child identities, then publish
   each area once under explicit spatial and runtime authority. Graph placement
   candidates alone do not claim physically compatible ground.
3. Bind ongoing native controllers, damage/rewards and renderer/query consumers
   to their retained owners. Existing events must continue, not merely birth.
   Removing player-facing zones does not authorize dropping event mechanics.
4. Persist and stream complete owners, including all content, histories, memory,
   unfinished events and random continuation. Prove leave/return and cold Continue
   over two live neighboring areas without re-birth or content loss.
5. Bind true biome regions and native content-area identities, then connect local
   revealed terrain, area/event knowledge and regional map zoom to that same
   world. Square MassHierarchy cells are streaming aids, not the required mapping.

No full parity or unlimited live-content claim follows from the finite proofs.
Placement/exploration continues separately; preserve its concurrent work.
The abandoned issuer draft with empty graphs, disabled holdfast and omitted
complexes remains unsuitable.
