# Native birth lifetime and immediate admission

Ongoing objective and rescue births keep the area's actual population factory,
raw actor census and native insertion order. Admission uses private creation
receipts from that population's concrete native factory, bound to an exact
synchronous operation frame. Selecting a factory does not give a caller a
reusable permission to create bodies in a later operation.

## Creation and insertion

The selected live population callable keeps its original receiver and is chosen
before its arguments are evaluated. The concrete factory records bodies in a
private creation frame. A wrapper cannot substitute an already-created body,
a body from another population, or an overridable attestation method.

A fresh body remains unclaimed before physical insertion. Eight explicit hooks
follow the native actors.push calls in ongoing objective, wave and rescue paths.
Each hook admits that exact inserted body before subsequent native effects.
This includes effects during the next wave creation and rescue name dressing.
The hooks are deliberate lifecycle additions; their claim is not receiver-only
algorithm equivalence. Ordinary World hosts have no optional admission hook.

Controllers sharing the same actual area share its operation stack. Only the
current top frame can use its selected factory closure. Successful nested
procession creation can pass unused receipts to its active parent; both the
child operation and final admission must have succeeded. Failure revokes unused
receipts. Genuine bodies already inserted before an error remain claimed, with
native prior writes intact. The finalizer never claims arbitrary census arrivals.

## Asynchronous boundary

Operations must finish synchronously. The owner first remembers own or inherited
then descriptor presence without invoking its getter. It then reads the public
result.then once at the existing check and preserves its exact thrown error.
Remembered descriptor presence causes conservative refusal even if the getter
deletes itself, removes its prototype, or returns a noncallable value. Refusal
happens before successful completion, so unused child receipts cannot transfer
to their parent.

Observation happens after the complete synchronous stack expires. A captured
Promise intrinsic can observe ordinary same-realm and cross-realm Promises with
a throwing or noncallable public then. Generic thenables use their once-selected
callable with Reflect.apply. Constructor and species lookup therefore occurs
after the scopes expire. Native Actor, array and ordinary result identities stay
unchanged.

This is not universal Promise branding. A completely prototype-stripped Promise
with no recognizable then descriptor is outside this contract. Arbitrary Proxy
traps, replaced global intrinsics and hostile Promise constructors or species
are not sandboxed. The APIs revoke their own scoped permissions; they cannot
revoke arbitrary asynchronous writes through raw references.
