# Native area displacement and movement restrictions

NativeSceneDisplacement shares the original impulse, grab release, cling release,
teleport, mount and movement-restriction rules with World. A retained
NativeAreaSceneDisplacement uses its actual scene census, native geometry,
actor lookup and campaign clock. This groups connected behavior so a push can
release a grip or cling, and an unhorse can update its rider relationship before
applying the native impulse.

The owner requires physical area admission and its exact registered displacement
service. Coordinates and copied actor IDs cannot confer authority. Native calls
between operations recheck admission, so a body transferred during an earlier
effect cannot receive a subsequent operation from its old area. Effects already
applied retain their native partial-failure order. Hidden carried flesh is not
an admitted physical body.

The actual mutable census, flashes, actor references, live method receivers and
geometry policies are retained. Mount seating may reorder that census exactly
as the original implementation does. No filtered copy receives those changes.
The original impulse limits, weight and authority calculations, status effects,
random consumption, channel priorities and motion restrictions stay authoritative.

This group does not implement full moveActor, boundary recovery, environment
damage, ram damage, controller ticking or the scheduler. It cannot substitute
for the objective watcher's complete movement provider. Detached payload casters
and cross-area relationship transfers still need their explicit lifecycles.

Native teleportActor is a combat displacement operation; sharing it does not add
player-facing area-border teleports. The intended world remains continuous,
with bounded and open places and meaningful obstacle navigation following
complete retained content ownership and publication.
