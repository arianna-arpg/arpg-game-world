# Campaign arrivals and Odyssey encounter refresh

Retained areas keep their live actors, terrain, rewards and objective state. Native content whose eligibility changes after first exploration must therefore run its own arrival operation. These changes extend the retained journey work without regenerating the whole area or moving local effects through another area's published writer.
