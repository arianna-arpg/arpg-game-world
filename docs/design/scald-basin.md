## 1. Thesis — the kettle country

The Scald Basin is the land as a kettle: a hydrothermal basin where the deep
heat never reaches the surface as melt — it arrives as WATER. Boiling
springs, sinter terraces in mineral prismatics, mudpots, steam banks, and
geysers that erupt **on a beat you can learn**. Yellowstone by way of
Southsun Cove, in the game's own register.

The one-sentence identity, and the axis every design choice hangs on:

> **The volcanic country is the fire's violence; the Scald Basin is the
> fire's PATIENCE.** Volcanic ground threatens constantly and randomly —
> vents pepper, lava stands, the danger is a property of the ground. Basin
> ground is SAFE almost always and lethal on schedule — the danger is a
> property of TIME, and the country's whole skill test is reading it.

That inversion is what earns the biome-type (§7), what the fauna teach
(§8), and what the player kit inherits (§9). The Warfront comparison makes
it sharpest: the Warfront's shellfire is hostile and arrhythmic — a gun
deciding, `cadence: [min,max]` re-rolled per shot, and the objective is to
SILENCE it. The basin's rain is indifferent and metronomic — the earth
breathing — and it cannot be silenced, only read. Same delivery grammar,
opposite relationship.

### What a country IS at HEAD (the survey)

- **A biome row** (`world/biomes.ts BIOMES`): patron faction, mapColor,
  label, spacing, `meld`, a climate claim (envelopes over temperature /
  moisture / wildness / elevation — the butteland and highland rows show
  elevation-claimed countries), `allowedLayouts`, layoutParams, landmark
  and structure chances.
- **A `BIOME_FIELD` weight** — staged countries run 1.7–2.3 ("staged faces
  need the acreage": desert 2.3, mountain 1.9, karst 1.9, garden 1.7);
  spectacle biomes run rarer (butteland 1.2 — "unmistakable when the tables
  rise").
- **Faces staged by `depthAffinity`** over biomeDepth in
  `data/tilesets.ts` — the garden/desert model. Warfront: grindfields
  `{to:.62}` → siegefront `{from:.42}`. Grove: meadow rim → deepwood
  `{to:.75}` → heartwood `{from:.55}` with glimmervale threading the band
  and the gleamhollow den off-frontier. Volcanic: cinderlands rim →
  caldera `{from:.35}` → wyrmfields `{from:.7, forceLayout:'massif'}`.
  Faces PIN recipes via `forceLayout` where the face IS a recipe (the
  karst precedent).
- **A kit file** (single-file doctrine: `data/garden.ts`,
  `data/merelake.ts`, `data/warfront.ts`) + painters where new doodad
  kinds need them + `data/doodadVisuals.ts` rows.
- **The trimmings the grove landing enumerated** (memory
  `grove-country-firefly-pass`): meld in `data/melds.ts`, lore rows,
  faction + BOTH name mills (`monsterNames.ts` + `nemesis.ts` — a fielded
  faction needs its tongue), boot imports, a probe + roster row same

## 3. THE BEAT LAW — geysers as terrain that keeps time

Her ruling: eruptions on TIMERS, the track fabric's timing grammar as
terrain. The load-bearing precedent is `engine/tracks.ts trackPose`: a
rider's pose is a PURE FUNCTION of the synced clock — deterministic across
seats, resumes and replays BY CONSTRUCTION, no state, no rng stream. Phase
offsets come from pure hashes (the `shatter`/`reversal` family: lane
geometry + salt, "every seat and every resume agree"). The SWEPT BEAT
sub-samples between beats so fast surfaces never tunnel.

**The geyser fixture generalizes exactly that clock, not the track.** A
geyser is a stationary emitter — it has no lane, no arc table, no riders —
so wedging it into `TrackSpec` (a one-point path with pauses) would contort
both fabrics. Proposed instead: a small new fabric, **`engine/geysers.ts`**,
whose whole law is borrowed:

- **GeyserSpec rows** (authored via the kit file, `ZoneTheme.geysers`,
  landmark builders, or a runtime ensure — the tracks authoring trio):
  `{ pos, class, band?, telegraph, column, downstream? }` — the CLOCK
  lives on the BAND (her second-walk shape, below); a bandless vent
  carries its own `period`/`phase` (the marsh one-off, a lair's
  set-piece vent).
- **THE CLOCK IS PURE — at BAND grain (her second-walk shape)**:
  eruption state at time t = pure f(zone clock, the band's period and
  phase); band clocks are ROLLED AT MINT from the zone stream, never at
  runtime. NO per-eruption jitter — this is still the anti-bombard
  (`BombardSpec.cadence` re-rolls per shot to feel "nearly random"; a
  geyser clock NEVER re-rolls: the looseness she asked for comes from
  GENERATION — the band layout and the band-clock rolls — not from dice
  at runtime, so the field stays learnable and co-op/resumes agree by
  construction). A runtime drift dial is possible but costs exactly that
  readability — flagged for her M0 walk, default off. The wire ships
  nothing but what the tell-wire idiom already ships for derived scalars.
- **DRAWN == TESTED, and THE BROIL LAW (ruled at the second walk)**: the
  telegraph is the water ITSELF beginning to BROIL at the vent — the roil
  rises over `telegraph` seconds (~2s DIAL — her "two-seconds early"),
  the steam thickens, the hiss lifts; drawn phenomena only, no countdown
  ring by default (the show-don't-tell law; `imminentThreatTo` still
  speaks so dodge-AI and the reticle agree with the eyes). THE ONE DRAWN
  WORD: this same broil is the game's whole vocabulary for "this water
  is about to hurt you" — the lake's deep middle (§6) wears it
  PERMANENTLY (a warning that never resolves is a refusal), a vent wears
  it for two seconds (imminent now). Learn it once, read it everywhere.
  The column's hit surface is the drawn column.
- **THE COLUMN**: at the beat, a strike at the fixture's own seat —
  mitigated typed fire ("scald" texture, §9) through the ordinary damage
  pipeline, plus an AUTHORLESS shove up/out (`engine/mass.ts` already names
  "wind, geysers" as the authorless push case — the law was reserved for
  exactly this). Standing in the throat when it blows is the country's
  honest worst idea.
- **CREDIT: uncredited environment** (the track fabric's `credit`-absent
  law verbatim; the trapworks doctrine — "the dead build no allegiance":
  the column and its rain spare NO faction). Kill credit follows the
  pushActor/pitfall precedent where a shover exists; here none does.
- **DORMANT SLEEPERS SPARED** where the sky posture says so (§4) — the
  sentry law holds.

**Vent classes** (DIAL, all): `hiss` (small, fast clocks ~12–18s,

**THE IGNITION DIAL (backlog #206 — the reserved seam, opened here).**
creep.ts's standing law, verbatim: "player casts never ignite fronts that

### THE LAKE — a new zone type (ruled), the mere generalized

**What the river type established** (`world/relief.ts` + `world/courses.ts`
+ the riverland recipe): a world-level feature hands zones MINT HINTS — the
course forces `forceLayout: 'riverland'`, orients the carve via
`riverSides` so consecutive zones read as one flow, and parameterizes it
(`riverLiquid`, causeways, isles) — the zone CONFORMS to a feature that
outranks it. **What the mere established** (memory `meadow-lake-pass`,
`data/merelake.ts`): a water body as a REGION FAMILY (shore / water /
fading / bed), carved classify-before-paint and ALL-OR-NOTHING (a failed
carve leaves the zone byte-flat — no orphan story), with authored
population (`packSplit: 0` — no surface packs dealt into a set piece) and
an optional condition-held phase grammar (the span fabric). THE LAKE is
those two precedents married at surface scale:

- **THE CONFORM LAW**: a `lake` layout recipe (registered like riverland —
  it auto-joins genqa). One very large centrally-focused water stamp —
  a wobble-rimmed blob (the mere's rim law; never a circle), sized so the
  LAND is an annulus: the zone's walkable body is the RING. Cells are
  CLASSIFIED before any paint (deep core / shallow shelf / shore / land),
  all-or-nothing; exits, portal clears, and reachability live on the ring
  (standing genqa invariants gate it for free). Zone size runs LARGE
  (~[3600,4600] × [2600,3400] — DIAL): the lake must read as geography,
  not a pond in a field — the lakelands recipe already owns "ponds in a
  field" (below).
- **THE TRAVERSABILITY RING**: partially traversable BY WATER GRADE —
  a wadeable shallow shelf rings the deep core (standStatus wading, the
  water row's own grammar), with spits, bars and isles reaching inward
  (the riverland `isles` layoutParam grammar — perch/loot pockets that
  join `ctx.pois`), and pool-and-terrace clusters at the waterline. You
  can get WET and get PARTWAY; the middle you cannot have.
- **THE DEEP-MIDDLE REFUSAL** (ruled: blocked because too deep). Two legal
  shapes exist in the region grammar — deep water that permits swimming
  but drains breath (`survival: { resource: 'breath' }`, the deep_water
  row), or ground that refuses entry outright. **Ratified (card 1): a
  `deepPolicy` dial per lake row** (`'block' | 'swim'`), **the sulphur
  debut `block`** — the middle visibly
  BOILS — THE BROIL LAW's permanent face (§3): the same drawn roil a
  vent wears for two seconds before bursting, worn here FOREVER (a
  warning that never resolves is a refusal; nobody asks why they can't
  swim water that is cooking) — drawn as water, refused as ground, shots passing (an occlusion-free firing lane across the middle
  is the lake's tactical signature — artillery duels across water no body
  can cross). A cold-country lake minted later flips the dial to `swim`
  and inherits breath pricing from standing law.
- **THE DEBUT IS SULPHUROUS**: the water wears the caustic palette
  (yellow-green shallows banding to mineral cyan at depth), the shelf
  stings (the sulphur_pool row's milder cousin — wading the shallows is a
  priced choice, not a stroll), shoreline crusts and terrace shelves in
  prism color, geysers standing IN the shallows (the new fixture kind's
  own placement law, §3), and the great lake's own metronome — ONE
  landmark-grade `great` vent offshore on an isle or shoal, the zone's
  loudest clock.
- **HOW IT JOINS WORLDGEN** (ratified — card 1): the debut join
  is IN-COUNTRY — the sulphur pools face pins the recipe the way karst
  faces pin theirs (`forceLayout: 'lake'` on the heart-face roll, or a
  high-weight variant), so the heart of every Scald Basin holds its lake.

  `RELIEF_CFG.trace` ends a river run "in a basin no probe escapes (a

  DIE in scald country — the geography will look intentional years before

- **BOUNDARY LAWS** (named so nobody re-litigates them): the lake is

## 10. THE SCORCH BAR (RULED at the walk, 2026-08-20 — her shape; the row proposal retired)

Her ruling, verbatim-near: rather than committing a new bespoke row,
**leverage what already exists with a twist** — the desert already runs a
heat mechanic (the swelter bake); convert it into a BAR like the light or
breath bars, **polarity INVERTED: the HIGHER the bar, the more scorched
the entity/player becomes**. In the DESERT it rises slowly and steadily
simply by existing there; in the SCALD it rises only from the hazards —
geyser eruptions, being near something like a sulphur pool — so the
scald's counterplay is positional: avoid the hazards and eruptions and the
bar can potentially always stay empty. One existing mechanic STANDARDIZED
instead of numerous bespoke ones.

The encoded shape:

- **ONE meter, working id `scorch` (name/color DIAL)** — the survival
  fabric's first FILL-polarity row: it RISES toward bad. **FULL IS NOT A
  CLIFF (re-shaped at the fourth walk — her ruling): no lethal ramp, no
  cook.** The bar's whole effect is PROGRESSIVE: the higher the bar, the
  more severe the scorch, the more FIRE RESISTANCE is stripped — an
  accumulating pressure over time, deliberately distinct from the
  light/breath bars that trigger on depletion like timers. Standing
  truth already carries the spine (one unit == one `sunscorched` stack,
  each stack −5% fire res, DIAL) — the amendment DELETES the full-state
  ramp and keeps the erosion. THE EIGHTHS (sealed at the fifth walk): the
  bar splits into eighths — one stack per ~12% of progress, eight at 100%
  — and AT FULL the player also wears heatstroke (the move + attack/cast
  penalty — ruled KEEP); never a life tick, ever.
  The reserved "future warmth meter" seat in `world/regions.ts` is SPENT
  by this: the vignette spec (warm-red family — a SEVERITY wash engaging
  above a frac, no dying squeeze), the HUD survival loop, and the
  `survivalEase_*` family all generalize — ease slows the RISE ('of the
  Lampkeeper'-style affixes arrive as one data line each).
- **THE DESERT REFIT**: `ZoneTheme.swelter`'s open-sun sunscorch bake
  becomes the bar's AMBIENT source — slow, steady, existence-priced,
  scaled by baked climate temperature exactly as today — and the standing
  `sunscorched`/`heatstroke` statuses re-seat as the bar's BANDS (the
  per-unit erosion status, the full-state slow — no ramp), so every
  existing consumer (douses, overlays, the shimmer fast-lane) keeps its
  meaning. THE REFIT LAW (amended at the fourth walk): rates, onset and
  reliefs stay identical — only VISIBLE now — while the EFFECT follows
  her ruling (the erosion spine kept, the cook never ported). (This is
  the one cross-country hunk; the Salt Flats seam note in the header
  applies.)
- **THE SCALD'S SOURCES (zero ambient — the ruled counterplay)**: the
  eruption column and plume, the burn rain's hits, sulphur-pool
  PROXIMITY (a near-field trickle — her "being near", not just wading),
  and (DIAL, leaning yes) the Char's standing fire fronts. Scald mist
  banks deliberately do NOT feed it (leaning — concealment stays pure,
  and the always-avoidable law prefers point sources over drifting ones).
  Every source is a drawn phenomenon — the bar agrees with the eyes.
- **RECOVERY**: the standing douse loop reversed into the bar — water and
  shade BLEED it, desert night bleeds it — plus a steady out-of-source
  decay (DIAL): the desert keeps its day/night breathing, the scald
  empties between mistakes.
- **ENTITIES WEAR IT TOO** (her words: "the entity/player") — and THE
  BASKER (§8) is the first kin whose AI READS its own: enrage keyed to
  the warm band, cold damage quenching it early, the tells fabric drawing
  body tint + posture off the SAME map the AI reads (drawn == tested, the
  tells law verbatim). And because the bar strips fire res on WHOEVER
  wears it — faction-blind — a basking kin's warm window is ALSO its
  fire-vulnerable window by construction (plates open + res down: the
  compounding law, free). The future deepwinter COLD bar is this row's
  mirrored sibling — the standardization pays twice, exactly her
  argument for it.
- **THE COMPOSITION LAW (her observation at the fifth walk, recorded as
  law)**: the survival bars COMPOSE and multiply — scorch governs the
  day, the light bar the night under an active Gloaming, so one walker
  can be scorched, heatstruck AND gnawed by the dark at once; "this
  entire functionality branches into multiples based on the existing
  mechanics." Each bar keeps a DISTINCT mechanic texture (breath = a
  timer into drowning; light = a timer into the dark's gnaw; scorch = an
  accumulator into erosion + a slow, never life) — the multiplication is
  the intent, and any future bar (the deepwinter cold — the fill-row
  fabric's mirrored sibling) should bring its OWN consequence texture
  rather than clone scorch's.
- **Residual DIALS only, no card owed**: rise/decay rates, the per-unit
  fire-res erosion (−5%/unit — "fine", her word: blessed as shipped),
  the source list's edges (mist? Char fronts?), display name and color.
  The heatstroke-at-full word is answered: KEEP.
