## Install

**Already playing a 0.5 build? You don't need this page.** Open the launcher and press **Update & Restart**. It downloads this release with a live progress readout, installs it by itself and relaunches the game. On Linux it swaps the new AppImage over the same file, so a Steam shortcut pointing at it keeps working.

**This is the last update you will ever fetch by hand.** See the next section.

**Windows (fresh install).** Download `HollowWake-Setup-0.6.0.exe` below and run it. It is a normal game installer: pick a folder (it defaults to a per-user install, no admin rights needed) and it puts a **Hollow Wake** shortcut on the desktop and in the Start Menu, plus an uninstaller entry in *Apps & features*. Windows SmartScreen may warn that the publisher is unknown, because the build is unsigned. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Linux / Steam Deck (fresh install).** Download `HollowWake-0.6.0-x86_64.AppImage`, mark it executable (`chmod +x`) and run it. On the Deck, add it as a non-Steam game from Desktop Mode, or run the install one-liner from STEAM.md. Launching from Steam boots straight into the game.

**Saves from 0.5 do not carry over.** Two months of changes reshaped characters, the passive tree and the world itself, so this version starts accounts fresh and tells you why on first boot. Saves live in your per-user data folder (Windows: `%APPDATA%/Hollow Wake`), and installing, updating or uninstalling the app never touches them.

## Updates look after themselves now

Until today the launcher only ever looked for hand-cut releases like this one, so installed copies sat on 0.5.0 while nightly builds shipped where no launcher could see them. From 0.6.0 on:

- **The launcher follows nightly builds.** Every night that passes its checks becomes the build your launcher offers. A night that fails anything is never published, so it is never offered: you keep the last good night.
- **Prefer a slower pace?** Switch **Update channel** to **Stable** on the launcher page. It never downgrades you. It simply waits for the next hand-cut release.
- **Every download is verified** against the checksum GitHub publishes before anything runs. A damaged or incomplete download is deleted, never installed.
- **Every build is boot-tested before it is published.** The packaged game is started on Windows and Linux, including a full rehearsal of finding and verifying its own next update.
- **Steam Deck Game Mode updates quietly.** Game Mode boots straight into the game, so there was never a launcher to press Update on. Now the game checks in the background, downloads the new build beside the running one while you play and swaps it in. Your session is never interrupted. The next launch is the new version.

## Highlights since v0.5.0 (686 commits in two months)

**Between lives, you stand in Mu.** The menu between runs became a place. You drift as a wisp through a boundless dark where every class stands waiting as a vessel. Linger beside one to read its card and wake as it. Classes you have not earned stand cowled and nameless. Now and then a vessel wears an ember: the Immortal covenant, which is met in Mu and never picked from a list.

**The Last Mile rolls its legion.** The prologue now fields one of seven legions per account, each marching under its own Father. The reckoning is played, not watched: the commander arrives, musters a ten-second cast, and you spend those seconds trying to stop it. At level 15 a revenge chain opens to hunt the Father who felled you.

**Classes are earned.** Every class beyond the starters hangs shrouded in the Vault, named only in runes. Its objectives read plain as you make progress toward them, and deeds in the field claim it. Mortal Essence buys mastery instead: four rungs per class that change the kit you wake with. Class after class now opens with complete branching skill trees, and each skill's tree unfolds in its own pane.

**Skills are things you carry.** Gems no longer drop bare. They arrive as Memories: pouches that remember what dropped them and where, recalled at the stone. Skills and supports live in your bag as items, all eight bar seats are drawn and trade places, and a dropped copy of a skill you already know swaps the old one out to your bag. Counters stock Memory pouches from your first day and buy them back.

**Lastlight grows with you.** The town climbs a ladder from hamlet to township as you raise its stations. The inn has rooms upstairs you can walk, its folk stroll between the furniture, and townsfolk talk about the weather, about each other and about what you killed. A notice board on the square posts bounties, and its expeditions charter new ground. The Reliquary is a side case for relics, gear that works only while it is seated. Town portals are a skill, never a scroll, and they remember their round trip.

**The map is a painted atlas.** The world map is drawn as relief: lit hills, snowlines, rivers tapering to their mouths, lakes in their basins. Peaks, ore lodes, lakes, canyons and calderas are map features, and the zones minted on them inherit what they promise. Volcanoes erupt on the world clock. Fortresses, lost cities and highland caverns unfold as connected regions with their own inner roads. And the map shows only what you have learned: nothing far away redraws it for you.

**A unique is a build.** Legends now grant skills you can bind and socket, fire their own triggers and keep their own state. Six chase relics read the geometry of the case they sit in. Super arts arrived too: ultimates that cut away to your own body as they fire, some paid for in kills through a gauge that fills as you fight.

**Enemies think in groups.** Magic packs share mechanics through visible links. Mixed-species crews take formation roles. Encounters pick plans, warn readably and commit. Stationary arena bosses fight in choreographed patterns. Cavalry rides into battle and gets thrown when the steed dies. Apex natives claim lairs, from a yeti den under the mountains to a labyrinth whose bull hunts by your trail until water breaks it. Sentries climb a visible ladder of suspicion, so you can creep past a sleeper or lure a watchman off his post.

**Your beasts grow up.** Tamed companions level with their keeper and take standing orders: aggressive, defensive or passive, shifted from the Tame skill itself. Downed allies and companions are revived by standing in their ring.

**Combat reads without reading.** Fights explain themselves through what you see and hear, not through captions. Statuses speak on the body that wears them, hits and deaths speak their material, enemies wear their internal state as visible tells, and emerging undead disturb the soil before they rise.

**New ground.** The Scald Basin keeps a beat: geysers, a great lake and a crone's cistern, under an hour when the whole country surges. Resources can be harvested in the field. Dying now ends in a rise and a shatter as the epilogue fades in.

**Smaller things that add up.** One Menu button on the HUD fans out every page and tells you when something wants your attention. Station dialogs that would overlap bind into one tabbed book. The bag sorts, items lock, and sell and salvage modes cancel quickly. A Wardrobe holds account-wide cosmetics that never touch combat. Panels can be moved if you opt in.
