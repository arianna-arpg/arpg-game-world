# Launcher CI repair — October 11, 2026

The launcher branch publisher requires a successful source check, the complete
fast green probe roster, a production build, and an Electron boot/save-isolation
smoke. Its old 25-minute job budget was shorter than the approximately one-hour
seamless probe run. Main commit ea56c7ac raises that budget to 120 minutes and
keeps every gate. The probe runner now prints bounded assertion/error tails when
a failed probe has no explicit FAIL line, including failures in parallel CI.

Seamless repairs are verification-only and are prepared outside the active
seamless checkout. They do not change combat, generation, streaming, saved data,
visuals, or content admission. No unfinished seamless content is merged into Main.
