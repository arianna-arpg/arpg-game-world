The held breath.

Plates latch. Veils fold. Flasks tip. Crystals split. Learn the preparation and release of a skill through its silhouette and motion, then give every skill the same color and hide the names.

Signature studies use enlarged shapes and normalized playback time. Instant skills skip preparation. Held guards show their stance; a damaging bash still requires its actual arming conditions. Offerings retain their established consumption signatures.
In play, preparation follows the real cast clock, and destructive releases appear only where fuel is spent. Stored damage and buff-duration indicators remain independent.
